## Privacy notice.
### What we collect
When you send an enquiry, apply for the mentorship programme, join the book waiting list or subscribe to updates, we store the details you enter in the form, such as your name, email address and message.

Enquiries are used to respond to your request. Mentorship applications are used only to assess your place in the programme and, if you are accepted, to send payment and onboarding information. Waiting list details are used to send updates about the book.

From founder-dependent to future-ready: building a firm that outlasts its founders — Modupe Olusoga
[Skip to content]
Most professional services firms are built around remarkable individuals. The harder work is designing an institution that can thrive without them.
This is sample text so the article layout can be reviewed. Replace it from the admin with the final, approved article.
### The challenge
Institutions are rarely undone by a single decision. They are shaped by the habits that accumulate when growth outpaces structure: decisions that depend on one person, knowledge that lives in a few heads, and a culture that has never been written down.
> Building for longevity begins long before anyone plans to leave.
### What changes the outcome
Clarity about what the organisation is for. Governance that distributes decisions. Leadership pipelines that are deliberate rather than accidental. And people who are developed, not merely deployed.
### A closing thought
The work is rarely glamorous, but it compounds. Every structure put in place today is a decision the next generation will not have to make alone.

### The challenge
Institutions are rarely undone by a single decision. They are shaped by the habits that accumulate when growth outpaces structure: decisions that depend on one person, knowledge that lives in a few heads, and a culture that has never been written down.
> Building for longevity begins long before anyone plans to leave.
### What changes the outcome
Clarity about what the organisation is for. Governance that distributes decisions. Leadership pipelines that are deliberate rather than accidental. And people who are developed, not merely deployed.
### A closing thought
The work is rarely glamorous, but it compounds. Every structure put in place today is a decision the next generation will not have to make alone.
