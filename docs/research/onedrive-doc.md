A. UPCOMING MENTORSHIP PROGRAMME

Programme Proposition

For individuals ready to birth their goals and aspirations.

This is a focused six-month mentorship programme for individuals who have goals, ambitions or ideas they are serious about bringing to life and who are prepared to commit to the discipline, dedication and consistency required to make meaningful progress.

The programme is not intended simply to provide inspiration or motivation.

It is designed to create an environment of:

Clarity | Intentional Action | Accountability | Consistency | Execution

The journey is essentially:

Aspiration → Clarity → Action → Consistency → Results

Intended Audience

The programme is for individuals who:

Have meaningful personal, professional or entrepreneurial goals they want to pursue;

Know they are capable of more but need greater clarity, structure and accountability;

Are prepared to do the work required between sessions;

Want access to mentorship, perspective and a structured community;

Are willing to be challenged and held accountable; and

Can commit consistently to the programme for six months.

Duration

February – July

The formal programme will run for six months.

Opening Session

There will be a special in-person Opening/Orientation Session in mid-January ahead of the formal commencement of the programme.

This session will establish individual goals, expectations, programme commitments and the foundation for the six-month journey.

Weekly Sessions

Every Thursday | 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Sessions will focus on reflection, learning, execution, accountability and progress against agreed goals.

Final Session

The programme will conclude with an in-person final session in July to review progress, consolidate learning, celebrate outcomes and determine participants' next steps.

Investment

₦200,000 per participant

Cohort Size

Participation will be deliberately limited to allow for meaningful interaction, accountability and mentorship.

Admission Process

Because dedication and consistency are fundamental to the programme, participation should be application-based rather than simply open registration.

The primary website CTA should therefore be:

APPLY FOR THE MENTORSHIP PROGRAMME

Applicants should provide information on their goals, what they want to achieve within six months, why they want to participate and their ability to commit to the programme schedule.

Successful applicants can then receive payment and onboarding information.

Suggested Website Message

You already have the aspiration. Are you ready to do the work?

This six-month mentorship experience is for individuals ready to move beyond intention and deliberately pursue the goals they have been carrying.

Because goals are not achieved by desire alone.

They require clarity.

They require action.

They require accountability.

And, above all, they require dedication and consistency.

Limited Cohort | February–July | ₦200,000

Apply for the Programme
