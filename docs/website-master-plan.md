# Modupe Olusoga — Complete Website Plan

Consolidated on 14 October 2026 from the questionnaire and our planning/design discussions.

This document describes the complete intended website. Features marked as planned have not been implemented. Later modules can be activated progressively as their content and launch details become available.

## 1. Purpose and brand positioning

The website will be the central digital platform for Modupe Olusoga's executive leadership, thought leadership, speaking, coaching, mentorship, writing and impact work.

Preferred positioning:

**Institutional Leader | Professional Services Executive | Author | Speaker | Certified Coach**

Two connected ideas underpin the platform:

1. Building institutions that thrive beyond individuals and their founders.
2. Helping people move from aspiration to intentional execution and sustainable outcomes.

The website should position her first as an institutional leader and thinker. Her professional roles and affiliations provide evidence of her experience.

### Primary audiences

- Founders, managing partners and senior leaders of law firms and professional services organisations.
- Boards and executives concerned with governance, succession and organisational effectiveness.
- Business professionals in law firms and professional services.
- Conference organisers, professional bodies and organisations seeking speakers or facilitators.
- Professionals and emerging leaders seeking mentorship, coaching and development.
- Readers, podcast audiences, collaborators and strategic partners.

### Priorities at launch

Establish authority and visibility, with clear pathways to:

- Invite Me to Speak.
- Apply for the Mentorship Programme.
- Book a Session / submit a session request.
- Explore My Insights.

Book and podcast activity becomes more prominent as their launches approach.

### Content editing and publication

- Draft, preview, publish, unpublish and archive where applicable.
- Keep the published version visible while edits are being prepared.
- Track revisions and support restoration.
- Feature selected articles, testimonials, programmes or media.
- Validate required content before publication or activation.
- Hide empty sections and unavailable modules.
- Redirect old published URLs when slugs change.
- Keep source/permission notes and internal review notes private.
- Choose fonts/colours through shared design settings; arbitrary code/CSS editing is not part of the initial admin.

### Admin roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Full content, settings, team and submission access |
| Editor | Public content and media; no private applicant data or user administration by default |
| Coordinator | Enquiries, applications, assignments and internal notes |

Secure login, password reset, login throttling and server-enforced permissions are required. Two-factor authentication is planned for privileged accounts. There is no public admin registration.

## 7. Operational workflows

### Mentorship admission

**Submitted → Under review → Accepted / Waitlisted / Declined → Onboarding → Enrolled**

Support withdrawal where appropriate. Track payment separately: not requested, pending or confirmed. Successful applicants receive payment and onboarding instructions. Preview and explicitly send decision messages; a status change should not silently send a rejection email.

### Engagement enquiries

**New → Reviewing → Awaiting information → Approved / Declined → Scheduled → Completed / Archived**

Save submissions before notifications. The admin inbox retains the request if email delivery fails.

### Book availability

**Waiting list → Preorder → Purchase**

Activate each stage only when its content and operational setup are ready.

## 8. Technical foundation and reliability

Existing stack: Laravel, Livewire, Blade, Tailwind and Vite. Local development uses Laravel Herd and SQLite. Production database, hosting, storage and delivery providers remain to be selected.

Reusable records should power multiple locations. An article or testimonial featured on the homepage should be the same record used on its detail page.

Core planned records: users/roles, profile/settings, pages/sections/revisions, articles/categories, testimonials, media, speaking topics/engagements, programmes/cohorts/applications, enquiries and initiatives. Add books, subscribers, podcast episodes and resources as their phases are implemented.

Required foundations:

- Form validation, spam controls, submission throttling and duplicate-submit handling.
- Clear loading, validation, success and error states.
- Private storage/access for application data and exports.
- File type/size validation and safe rich-text handling.
- Notification queues and retries.
- SEO metadata, social sharing images, sitemap and redirect management.
- Accessible keyboard interaction, readable contrast and mobile layouts.
- Image optimisation and appropriate loading behaviour.
- Backups, restore procedures and configurable retention/deletion.
- Credentials managed through deployment configuration, rather than public content fields.

| Phase | Scope |

| 1 — Brand, authority & mentorship | Homepage, About, Insights, Speaking, Programmes, Impact, Media and Contact; enquiries, mentorship applications, blog/testimonials and core admin |
| 2 — Book & audience building | Dedicated book page, waiting list, later preorder, newsletter/community, resources and enhanced media |

Within Phase 1, continue designing/building the homepage section by section, then complete destination pages, admin editing and the application/review systems. Validate responsive design and core interactions as they are built.

Launch only content-ready modules. New components should be activated progressively instead of delaying everything until every asset exists.

Checkout requires defined payment, order, refund and print/digital fulfilment arrangements. A learning-management system, participant portal and full CRM are separate future scope unless requested.

## 12. Definition of launch readiness

The owner can update key content without code; public pages show only approved material; primary journeys work; private submissions stay private; notification failures do not lose requests; application availability reflects cohort settings; desktop/mobile layouts and keyboard interactions are checked; real assets replace temporary ones; and production configuration, policies and backups are ready.
